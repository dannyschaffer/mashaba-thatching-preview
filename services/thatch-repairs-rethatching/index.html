Questions about thatch repairs & re-thatching

How do I know if my thatch needs re-thatching?

Leaks, thin or sagging patches, dark rotten thatch and holes at the top are the usual signs. Send us photos and we'll tell you.

Repair or re-thatch?

If the damage is in a few spots, a repair is enough. If the thatch is old and thin all over, a re-thatch lasts much longer. See our guide below.
