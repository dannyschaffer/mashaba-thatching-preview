Repair or Re-thatch? How to Tell What Your Thatch Needs

The signs to look for on a lapa or thatch roof, and when a repair is enough.

Send us a photo

Signs your thatch needs work

  • Water drips or stains under the roof after rain
  • Thin or sagging patches you can see light through
  • Dark, rotten or mossy thatch
  • Holes or gaps at the top of the roof
  • Thatch blown loose after a storm

When a repair is enough

If the damage is in one or two places and the rest of the thatch is still thick, a repair fixes it for much less. We replace the damaged thatch and blend it into the old.

When to re-thatch

If the thatch is thin and old all over, patching only moves the leak. A full re-thatch strips the old grass and lays a new coat, so the roof works and looks like new.

Questions

How long does a thatch roof last?

A well-built thatch roof that is looked after lasts many years. Thin thatch, and the top of the roof, wear out first, faster in the coastal damp. [Joaquim to confirm typical lifespan.]

Is a repair cheaper than a re-thatch?

Usually, yes. A repair only replaces the damaged areas. But patching thatch that is old and thin all over just moves the leak, so then a re-thatch is better value.

Does thatch affect my insurance?

Often, yes. Many insurers ask about thatch and may need a fire-retardant treatment or a lightning conductor. Ask your insurer before the work starts.

When is the best time to repair or re-thatch?

Before the summer rain, so leaks are fixed before they damage the poles and frame underneath.
